The Heart of Hiroshi

Signature

Hiroshi Tonkotsu

$18

At the heart of our signature dish lies our meticulously crafted housemade Hakata-style Tonkotsu broth, slow simmered for hours from pork bones to achieve depth and richness. The umami-rich broth is infused with sea-salt and dashi, topped with braised Berkshire pork belly slices, and marinated soft-boiled egg create a satisfying bowl of ramen. The addition of lotus root, black mushroom, bamboo shoot, green onion, and pickled red ginger adds depth of flavor and texture. Served with thin straight noodles to complement all that flavorful broth.
