Our Story

Where Authentic Ramen Meets Rich Flavor

Ramen Hiroshi is a beloved Japanese kitchen serving handcrafted ramen built on slow-simmered broths, fresh ingredients, and traditional recipes inspired by Japan.

From our signature tonkotsu to bright yuzu bowls, seasonal specials, and comforting sides, we bring true Japanese comfort food to Walnut Creek, San Ramon, Emeryville, and San Francisco.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What are you known for?

Authentic Japanese ramen — rich tonkotsu, flavorful shoyu and shio, creamy tantanmen, and refreshing yuzu ramen — plus chicken and vegetarian bowls, crispy chicken karaage, wings, handmade gyoza, fresh salads, yakitori skewers, and our signature mochi ice cream.

What meals do you serve?

We serve lunch and dinner, seven days a week.

Do you offer delivery or takeout?

Yes — both takeout and delivery are available, along with easy online ordering at every location.

Where are you located?

You'll find us in San Ramon, Walnut Creek, San Francisco, and Emeryville — serving authentic Japanese ramen across the Bay Area.
